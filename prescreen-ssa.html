Social Security Administration Prescreen

We are not the Social Security Administration. We are only advocates to help you.

For Social Security Administration help, we do a prescreen first to see if you qualify to apply for Social Security Administration benefits. Answer what you can.

When you sign up, we add you to our list and tell you what number you are. We will determine if you can move on to the next step to apply for Social Security Administration benefits. An email will be sent to you with results.

This prescreen does not decide your case. Social Security Administration decides, in writing.
