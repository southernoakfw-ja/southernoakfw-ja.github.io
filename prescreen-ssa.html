Social Security Administration Prescreen

We are not the Social Security Administration. We are only advocates to help you.

For Social Security Administration help, we ask some questions first. This is called a prescreen. It helps us see if you can apply. Answer what you can.

When you sign up, we put you on our list and tell you your number.
We check your answers. Then we email you the results: you can move to the next step, or we need a little more information.

This prescreen does not decide your case. The Social Security Administration decides, in writing.

For Texas families only, please.
