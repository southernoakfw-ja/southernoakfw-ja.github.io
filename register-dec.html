Turning 18: What Changes

What to Do First — Start the New Year Ready — a free Zoom webinar with Jessica Alvarez, for parents & caregivers.

Friday, December 11, 2026 · 6:30–7:15 PM CT · Live on Zoom

For families whose teen turns 18 in 2027 — or soon after. At 18, Social Security switches to adult rules, the way income counts changes, and there is a benefit on a parent's own work record that most families never hear about. We cover what changes, what to do first, and the papers to gather over the holidays.

Only 20 spots. Register below and you will get your Zoom link right away — and you can add the class to your calendar so you don't forget. You can also send your question when you register, and Jessica will chat about it during the class.
