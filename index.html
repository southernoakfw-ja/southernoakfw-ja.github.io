Life Insurance

Life Insurance You Don’t Have to Die to Use

Most people think life insurance only pays when you die. Many of today’s policies can also help while you are living — that is called Living Benefits. If a qualifying illness or condition happens, you may be able to use part of your benefit early, when your family needs it most. I offer three kinds of coverage:

1. Term Life Insurance

Coverage for a set number of years — for example, while your children are growing up or while you are paying on your home. It is the simplest, lowest-cost way to protect your family’s income. Many term policies include Living Benefits.

2. IUL — Indexed Universal Life

Permanent coverage that can build cash value over time, tied to a market index without being invested directly in the market. An IUL can protect your family for life and may include Living Benefits. I will teach you exactly how it works — the good parts and the limits — before you decide.

3. Final Expense

A smaller, permanent policy made to cover funeral costs and final bills, so your family is not left raising money during the hardest week of their lives. Final expense is simple to apply for, and it is made to stay in place for life.

How it works with me:

  1. We start with your budget — what your family can truly afford.
  2. I teach you how each kind of coverage works, in plain words.
  3. I recommend the coverage that fits your family and your budget.
  4. You decide. No pressure, ever.

Important Questions

Is life insurance right for me?

If someone depends on your paycheck — a spouse, a partner, your children — life insurance is how you make sure they are protected if something happens to you, and Living Benefits can help while you are still living after a qualifying condition. We start with your budget, together, and you decide.

How much coverage do I need?

It depends on your family: your income, your home, your debts, and how long your family would need support. There is no one right number. In our free talk, we work it out together in plain words before any quote.

Is a medical exam required?

Many policies do not need a medical exam. What is needed depends on the type of coverage and the carrier’s rules, and I will tell you up front what to expect.

Can I choose my beneficiaries?

Yes. You choose who receives the benefit — most families name a spouse or partner first, and a backup beneficiary too — and you can update your choice as life changes.

How do I sign up?

Book a free consultation with me, or send in the quote form above. We will talk about your family, answer your questions, and choose the right coverage together.
