Thank You — Here Is Your Complete Package — Child

Your payment went through. Everything in your package is on this page — please save this page’s address, or download your worksheet now.

Step 1 — Download Your Worksheet

Your worksheet is a PDF. Save it or print it. It is yours. Work through it at your own pace.

Download Your Worksheet (PDF)

Step 2 — Create Your ID.me Account

For a child (ages 0–17), SSA looks at the parents’ income, and the ID.me account is created by the parent who will file, in that parent’s own name. Your packet shows you where to go, step by step. It also lists the papers to have ready. Follow the steps in your packet.

Step 3 — Fill Out Your Intake Form

When your ID.me is done, fill out your intake form and send it in. Answer each question correctly — most of the questions on the application come from your intake answers.

Fill Out Your Intake Form

Step 4 — Do the Online Application

Use the steps in your packet and do the application yourself, at your own pace. Most of the questions come from your intake answers. Answer each one correctly.

Save the Reference # SSA gives you. That is how you get back in.

Need Help?

If you get stuck on a step, email us at southernoakfw@gmail.com. If you want Jessica to guide you on Zoom while you file, ask about SSA Filing Help — she gives you the steps, you file, and she guides you, with up to 4 hours included.

Step 5 — Your SSA Phone Interview

After the application, SSA will send you a date for an interview by phone.

Watch for papers from SSA. Do not put them aside. They have deadlines, and they matter.

If You Are Denied — Do Not Let the Deadline Pass

If you get a denial letter, email us the same week at southernoakfw@gmail.com. You generally have only 60 days to appeal — do not let that deadline go by. We will connect you with special needs attorneys who can further assist you with your case. The attorney does not charge an upfront fee — they are paid only if you win.

Questions?

If anything is missing or a link does not open, email us at southernoakfw@gmail.com and we’ll make it right.
