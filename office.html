Life Insurance by Age — Quick Guide

When to use: a starting point when a client asks how much life insurance they need and for how long. The right amount always depends on their income, debts, and family — this guide starts the conversation.

⭐ Ages 20–29
30-year term — locks in low rates early
Face amount $250k–$500k
Why: Protects future income, student loans, young families, mortgage plans.

⭐ Ages 30–39
20–30-year term
Face amount $250k–$500k (sometimes $750k)
Why: Peak earning years, kids at home, mortgage responsibilities.

⭐ Ages 40–49
15–20-year term
Face amount $150k–$300k
Why: Kids nearing adulthood, debts decreasing, premiums rising.

⭐ Ages 50–59
10–15-year term
Face amount $100k–$250k
Why: Protect spouse, remaining debts, income replacement until retirement.
