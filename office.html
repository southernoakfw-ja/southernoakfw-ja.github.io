SSA Process of Application

Step-by-step help to apply for SSI, SSDI, or DAC benefits — for a child, or for a young adult who is turning 18.

How It Works — After You Buy

  1. Pay for your package. You will get your packet.
  2. Make your ID.me account. Your packet shows you where to go, step by step. It also lists the papers you need.
  3. Fill out the SSA intake form on our website: SSA Intake Form. Once you are done, email it to us at southernoakfw@gmail.com. Answer each question correctly — most of the questions on the application come from your intake.
  4. Do the online application yourself, with the steps in your packet. Take your time. Answer each question correctly.
  5. Save the Reference # SSA gives you. That number is how you get back in.
  6. Social Security will send you a date for a phone interview.
  7. Watch your mail and email for papers from Social Security. Do not put them aside. They have deadlines, and they matter.

For a child (ages 0–17): Social Security looks at the parents’ income. The parent who files makes the ID.me account.

For an adult child (ages 18–26): Social Security looks at only their income. The parents’ income does not count. The ID.me account is theirs, not the parent’s. If the adult child does not speak, or it is a DAC case, the parents apply for them.

Life Insurance — Covering Life, In Living and In Loss

Coverage that protects your family while you are living, and after you are gone — Term and Indexed Universal Life (IUL), explained in plain words.

Learn more — Term and IUL, explained in plain words

Real protection covers your family twice: while you are living, and when you are gone. Southern Oak helps families choose coverage with living benefits you can use during your lifetime, and a death benefit that protects the people you love after your passing — so one hard season never becomes a financial crisis. Term life insurance and Indexed Universal Life (IUL) options are explained in plain language, so you can choose the coverage that truly fits your family. We are E&O insured and licensed with PFA, National Life Group, and Foresters.

No fee until the client approves the policy

What Is Term Life Insurance?

Term covers you for a set number of years — like 10, 20, or 30 years. If you pass away during that time, it pays your family. It is usually the lowest-cost way to protect your family while children are growing up, or while a home is being paid off.

What Is an IUL?

An IUL (Indexed Universal Life) is coverage that can last your whole life. Part of what you pay builds cash value over time. The cash value grows based on a market index, with limits — it does not go up and down directly with the stock market. You may be able to use some of the cash value while you are living.

How They Help You as a Policy Holder

Both kinds of policies can include living benefits. If you become seriously ill — like a heart attack, stroke, or cancer — you may be able to use part of your benefit while you are still living, to help with bills and care. The part you use early is paid on a discounted basis, and it lowers the amount left for your family later. Life insurance is there for your family after you are gone — and, with living benefits, it can help you while you are here, too.

Learn more — how the program works

Prescription medications shouldn’t be a luxury — but for millions of Texans, the cost is a real barrier to staying healthy. Southern Oak helps individuals and families find prescription assistance programs (PAPs), manufacturer savings programs, and pharmacy discount options that can significantly reduce or eliminate out-of-pocket medication costs, and we guide you through the enrollment from start to finish.

About Jessica — Website Bio (Office Copy)

This bio lived on the public About page. On 2026-10-09 Jessica moved it here — the public website now shows Services and Book only.

Jessica Alvarez is the Founder, Owner, and Advocate of Southern Oak Financial & Wellness, based in San Antonio, Texas. She provides guidance to individuals and families navigating Social Security disability applications, medication assistance programs, and life insurance planning.

Jessica brings five years of experience serving the special needs community, including her work with ABC of Southwest Texas, along with two years of experience in medication assistance programs. She has served as a Special Needs Advocate with Bexar County–area organizations, including University Health and CommuniCare.

She has been a life insurance agent with PFA for four years, guiding people toward a better life.

For Jessica, this work is personal. At just 11 years old, she developed juvenile rheumatoid arthritis, and she still remembers what her family went through — the hardship her parents faced with SSA, the cost of medication, and the struggle for government assistance, often not knowing where to turn. She knows firsthand the struggles parents face with a child in need, because she lived them. That is why helping, advocating for, and supporting others is her life’s work — and why she built Southern Oak’s system: so families facing what hers faced have clear guidance, honest answers, and someone truly on their side. Those who know Jessica know this about her: she is honest, she is trusting, and she always gives if she can. She has thought her prices through with care, too, setting them at a level families can truly afford — because help people cannot afford is not really help.

She is currently a doctoral candidate in Education — Organizational Leadership, with a focus on special needs. Her advanced studies in leadership and systems design inform her practice: structured processes, clear communication, and advocacy that places families at the center of every decision.

Jessica founded Southern Oak on a simple conviction: no family should have to face complex benefits, medication, and insurance systems alone. Her work ensures that every client is met with professionalism, patience, and a clear plan — and that someone knowledgeable is always seated on their side of the table.

Everything Jessica offers is built around the help families need most. That is why she has taken the classes, earned the certifications, and pursued the degrees in her field — to make sure that families who cannot afford high prices still have a place to turn: a place where someone understands their need for help and has the knowledge to act on it. Jessica is serious about this work, and the families she serves know she means business — and that she stands behind them with real support.
