About Me

Listening First. Educating Always. No Surprises.

I'm Jessica Alvarez, Founder and Owner of Southern Oak Financial & Wellness in San Antonio, Texas, and a licensed, independent life insurance agent. I built my career serving families: five years working with the special needs community, including ABC of Southwest Texas; serving as a special needs advocate with Bexar County-area organizations, including University Health and CommuniCare; two years working with a medication assistance program; and serving as a benefit advocate with Consolidated Planning Group in Houston. I have been in life insurance for four years, and in April 2026 I founded Southern Oak Financial & Wellness so families could get this help from one person who knows their story.

Education matters in this work. I am a doctoral candidate in Education — Organizational Leadership, with a focus on special needs, and I keep learning so I can keep teaching clearly.

My passion for this work is personal. I was diagnosed with juvenile rheumatoid arthritis at 11, and I watched my parents carry the worry, the bills, and the paperwork that come with doctors, medication, and Social Security. That is why my vision is simple: to be the trusted advocate families turn to first, so no one navigates benefits, medication, or insurance alone.

My work is about listening, educating, and making sure my clients know exactly what they are getting. I listen first, because every family's story is different. I educate in plain words, because a plan you do not understand is not a plan. And before you agree to anything, we go through it together — what it covers, what it does not cover, and what it means for your family — with no pressure and no surprises.

This is your future life.
The choices you make today protect the people you love tomorrow, and you deserve to understand every step of the plan that protects it.
