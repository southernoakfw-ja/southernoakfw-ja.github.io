Meet Jessica Alvarez

Jessica Alvarez is the Founder, Owner, and Advocate of Southern Oak Financial & Wellness, based in San Antonio, Texas. She provides guidance to individuals and families navigating Social Security disability applications, medication assistance programs, and life insurance planning.

Jessica brings five years of experience serving the special needs community, including her work with ABC of Southwest Texas, along with two years of experience in medication assistance programs. She has served as a Special Needs Advocate with Bexar County–area organizations, including University Health and CommuniCare.

She has been a life insurance agent with PFA for four years, guiding people toward a better life.

For Jessica, this work is personal. At just 11 years old, she developed juvenile rheumatoid arthritis, and she still remembers what her family went through — the hardship her parents faced with SSA, the cost of medication, and the struggle for government assistance, often not knowing where to turn. She knows firsthand the struggles parents face with a child in need, because she lived them. That is why helping, advocating for, and supporting others is her life’s work — and why she built Southern Oak’s system: so families facing what hers faced have clear guidance, honest answers, and someone truly on their side. Those who know Jessica know this about her: she is honest, she is trusting, and she always gives if she can. She has thought her prices through with care, too, setting them at a level families can truly afford — because help people cannot afford is not really help.

She is currently a doctoral candidate in Education — Organizational Leadership, with a focus on special needs. Her advanced studies in leadership and systems design inform her practice: structured processes, clear communication, and advocacy that places families at the center of every decision.

Jessica founded Southern Oak on a simple conviction: no family should have to face complex benefits, medication, and insurance systems alone. Her work ensures that every client is met with professionalism, patience, and a clear plan — and that someone knowledgeable is always seated on their side of the table.
