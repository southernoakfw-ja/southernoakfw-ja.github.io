Meet Jessica Alvarez

Family Services Consultant & Advocate

I'm Jessica Alvarez, a licensed life insurance agent in San Antonio, Texas. I started Southern Oak Financial & Wellness to teach families how life insurance works — in plain words, one step at a time. Southern Oak is just me: when you call or write, you talk to me.

I do two things. I help families choose life insurance that fits their budget — Term, IUL, and Living Benefits. As an independent agent, I work for your family — not for one insurance company. And I make free Watch & Learn videos, so you can learn at your own pace before we ever talk.

This work is personal for me. When I was 11 years old, I got juvenile rheumatoid arthritis. I watched my parents carry the worry and the bills, not always knowing where to turn. I learned early what hard times feel like for a family with no plan. That is why I do this work: so your family has a plan in place before hard times come — and someone patient to explain every step.

I've been a life insurance agent for four years, working independently, and I serve families all across Texas — in English and Spanish. I'm also studying for my doctorate in Education, in Organizational Leadership. People who know me say I'm honest and kind. My belief is simple: every family deserves life insurance they understand, at a price they can afford — and someone on their side.
