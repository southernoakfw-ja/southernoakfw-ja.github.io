The advocate behind Southern Oak Financial & Wellness.

Meet Jessica Alvarez

Special Needs Disability Consultant & Advocate

Jessica Alvarez started Southern Oak Financial & Wellness in San Antonio, Texas. She is a Special Needs Disability Consultant & Advocate. She helps families with Social Security Administration benefits, medication help, life insurance, and credit repair — one step at a time. Her medication information and Social Security start form are free.

Jessica has worked with the special needs community for five years. She worked with ABC of Southwest Texas. She spent two years in medication assistance programs. She has been a Special Needs Advocate with groups in the Bexar County area, like University Health and CommuniCare. She has also been a life insurance agent with PFA for four years.

This work is personal for Jessica. When she was 11 years old, she got juvenile rheumatoid arthritis. She remembers what her family went through — the hard times with SSA, the high cost of medicine, and not knowing where to turn. She knows what parents go through, because she lived it. That is why she built Southern Oak: so families have clear steps, honest answers, and someone on their side.

People who know Jessica say she is honest and kind, and she gives when she can. She set her prices low on purpose, so families can afford help. Jessica is now studying for her doctorate in Education — Organizational Leadership, with a focus on special needs. Her belief is simple: no family should face these hard systems alone.

Jessica is currently a doctoral candidate in Education — Organizational Leadership, with a focus on special needs. She has taken the classes, earned the certifications, and pursued the degrees behind this work, so families who cannot afford high prices still have a place to turn — and someone knowledgeable always seated on their side of the table. Jessica founded Southern Oak on a simple conviction: no family should have to face complex benefits, medication, and insurance systems alone.
