Meet Jessica Alvarez

Family Services Consultant & Advocate

I'm Jessica Alvarez. I started Southern Oak Financial & Wellness in San Antonio, Texas. I'm a Family Services Consultant & Advocate, and I help families with life insurance — one step at a time. Southern Oak is just me — when you reach out, you talk to me.

Southern Oak focuses on two things: life insurance — Term, IUL, and Living Benefits — and Watch & Learn, my free teaching videos.

I've worked with the special needs community for five years — with ABC of Southwest Texas, two years in medication assistance programs, and as a Special Needs Advocate with groups like University Health and CommuniCare. I've also been a life insurance agent with PFA for four years.

This work is personal for me. When I was 11 years old, I got juvenile rheumatoid arthritis. I remember what my family went through — the hard times with SSA, the high cost of medicine, and not knowing where to turn. I know what parents go through, because I lived it. That is why I built Southern Oak: so families have clear steps, honest answers, and someone on their side.

People who know me say I'm honest and kind. I'm studying for my doctorate in Education — Organizational Leadership, with a focus on special needs. My belief is simple: no family should have to figure out life insurance alone, and every family deserves someone knowledgeable on their side.
