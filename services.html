Medication Assistance — Prescription Assistance Program

Prescription medications shouldn’t be a luxury — but for millions of Texans, the cost is a real barrier. Southern Oak helps individuals and families find prescription assistance programs (PAPs), manufacturer savings programs, and pharmacy discount options that can significantly reduce or eliminate out-of-pocket medication costs.

  • Manufacturer PAPs
  • Pharmacy Discount Programs
  • Program Research & Enrollment

If your medication is on our medication list, we can provide the forms for free.

If you require further assistance, book an appointment for a 30-minute session — $40.00.
