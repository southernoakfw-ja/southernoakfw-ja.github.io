Services

We plan your family, and your child's road to 18 and beyond. Southern Oak helps families protect what matters with life insurance, find low-cost medication you can afford, repair your credit for a better living, and file for Social Security (SSA) benefits — for your child, and for your adult child transitioning into adulthood (ages 18–26).

Proudly serving San Antonio, the Rio Grande Valley, and surrounding cities.

A small business owned company with a big heart.

What We Do

SSA Benefits

Step-by-step help to apply for SSI, SSDI, or DAC benefits — for a child, or for a young adult who is turning 18.

Life Insurance

Term and IUL coverage with Living Benefits — covering life, in living and in loss. No fee until you approve your policy.

Medication Assistance

A free check to lower what you pay for medicine. Forms are free if your medication is on our low-cost list.

Budget & Credit Repair

We analyze your credit, dispute what is wrong, and build a monthly budget that works — for a better living.

Transition to 18

Getting your young adult ready for adult benefits and adult life — decisions and papers done before the birthday.

Family Planning

One written plan for your whole family — what to work on first, your steps in order, every deadline written down.

What We Provide — How It Works

1 · Free Consultation
