Turning 18 changes everything in the Social Security system: a child who received benefits is reviewed under adult rules, and many young adults lose support simply because the transition was not prepared for. Whether you are starting a child’s claim or preparing for adulthood, begin by choosing your package below.

Medication Assistance — Prescription Assistance Program

Learn more — how the program works

Prescription medications shouldn’t be a luxury — but for millions of Texans, the cost is a real barrier to staying healthy. Southern Oak helps individuals and families find prescription assistance programs (PAPs), manufacturer savings programs, and pharmacy discount options that can significantly reduce or eliminate out-of-pocket medication costs, and we guide you through the enrollment from start to finish.

  • Manufacturer PAPs
  • Pharmacy Discount Programs
  • Program Research & Enrollment

If your medication is on our medication list, we can provide the forms for free.

Step 1 — Check GoodRx. Search your medication at www.goodrx.com to find free coupons and compare prices at pharmacies near you — you can print your coupon right from the website and take it to the pharmacy.

Step 2 — Check Cost Plus Drugs. Search their medication list at costplusdrugs.com for clear, low-cost pricing without the middleman markups.

Step 3 — Ask us about manufacturer assistance. Many brand-name medications have Patient Assistance Programs (PAPs) that provide medication free or at low cost to those who qualify. And if your medication is on our medication list, we can provide the forms for free.
