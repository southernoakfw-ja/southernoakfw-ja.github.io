SSA Application Assistance — For Your Disabled Child & the Transition to Adulthood

What We Help You With — Step by Step

Complete Package — Child (for SSI for a child under 18)

  • Step 1 — Protect your filing date: how and where to start the claim
  • Step 2 — The Child Disability Report: every condition, provider, and medicine to list
  • Step 3 — The SSI interview: household income, resources, and deeming, explained plainly
  • Step 4 — School records: what to request from the school, and how
  • Step 5 — The six areas of functioning: how to document your child’s daily life with real examples
  • Step 6 — The consultative exam: what to expect and how to prepare
  • Step 7 — The decision: what approval means, and how to appeal on time if denied

Complete Package — Adult (SSI, SSDI & DAC)

  • Step 1 — Know your programs: SSI, SSDI, and DAC — and which ones to ask for by name
  • Step 2 — Protect your filing date: how to start, and why the date matters for back pay
  • Step 3 — The Adult Disability Report: listing all of your conditions together
  • Step 4 — The Work History Report: your jobs from the last 5 years, described the right way
  • Step 5 — The Function Report: telling your daily-life story honestly and completely
  • Step 6 — Medical evidence and the consultative exam: keeping your case strong while you wait
  • Step 7 — The decision and appeals: timelines, the 60-day appeal window, and protecting your benefit after approval

Medication Assistance — Prescription Assistance Program

Prescription medications shouldn’t be a luxury — but for millions of Texans, the cost is a real barrier to staying healthy. Southern Oak helps individuals and families find prescription assistance programs (PAPs), manufacturer savings programs, and pharmacy discount options that can significantly reduce or eliminate out-of-pocket medication costs, and we guide you through the enrollment from start to finish.

  • Manufacturer PAPs
  • Pharmacy Discount Programs
  • Program Research & Enrollment

If your medication is on our medication list, we can provide the forms for free.

Rx Assist — Check If Your Medication Qualifies (Free)

Not sure if your medication qualifies for savings? You can check in just a few minutes — and this service is completely free, because Southern Oak cares for you.

Step 1 — Check GoodRx. Search your medication to find free coupons and compare prices at pharmacies near you.

Step 2 — Check Cost Plus Drugs. Search their medication list for clear, low-cost pricing without the middleman markups.

Step 3 — Ask us about manufacturer assistance. Many brand-name medications have Patient Assistance Programs (PAPs) that provide medication free or at low cost to those who qualify. And if your medication is on our medication list, we can provide the forms for free.
