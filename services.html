Our Services

Help for families in San Antonio, the Rio Grande Valley, and surrounding cities — virtual / online only.

A small business owned company with a big heart.

What We Do

Social Security Administration Application Guidance — We guide you step by step to apply for SSI, SSDI & DAC. Free. You get 2 free 30-minute sessions. If you need further assistance or guidance, it is $10.00 for every 30 minutes needed.
Medication Assistance — We help you find medication you can afford. Free.
Life Insurance Consultation and Quote — A clear talk about protecting your family. Free until you agree to a policy.
Credit Repair — We analyze your credit and prepare 1 dispute. $149.00.

We help 20 people a month on the free services. Book an appointment to get on the list — we will tell you what number you are. Once you are on the list for contact, an email will be sent to you.

Donations of $10.00 or more are welcome. They help us continue helping the community in need, to better support their child's future.

For medication assistance, we will also check if we are able to assist on any of our programs. You will receive an email. You get information sent to you. If you need assistance to fill out the application, a donation is requested.

How to Start

Fill out the intake and tap the button for your service. Then book your appointment — or send us your questions. Only credit repair has a fee, paid when you start.

For Social Security Administration, we do a prescreen first to see if you qualify to apply. See the Social Security Administration prescreen questions — there are questions for a child, and questions for 18 plus.

For credit repair, please know before you start: do not be late on payments, do not pull your credit for a purchase, and do not get new loans — or your services will be cancelled. What you need to get started is in the agreement.
