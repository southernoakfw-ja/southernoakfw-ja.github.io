Services

We plan your family, and your child's road to 18 and beyond. Southern Oak helps families protect what matters with life insurance, find low-cost medication you can afford, repair your credit for a better living, and file for Social Security (SSA) benefits — for your child, and for your adult child transitioning into adulthood (ages 18–26).

Proudly serving San Antonio, the Rio Grande Valley, and surrounding cities.

A small business owned company with a big heart.

What We Do

SSA Benefits — help applying for SSI / SSDI, step by step
Life Insurance — Term & IUL with Living Benefits; no fee until you approve a policy
Medication Assistance — a free check to lower what you pay
Budget & Credit Repair — a budget that works, and credit fixed step by step
Transition to 18 — ready for adult benefits and adult life, before the birthday
