Our Services

Help for low- and middle-income families in San Antonio, the Rio Grande Valley, and nearby cities. All visits are online, by Zoom and email.

Each month, we help 10–20 clients free of charge on the free services below. We will tell you what number you are in line, and we will email you when it is your time.

A small business with a big heart.

For families in San Antonio, the Rio Grande Valley, and nearby towns only, please.

What We Do

Social Security Administration Application Guidance — FREE

We help you step by step to apply for SSI, SSDI, and DAC. You get 2 free 30-minute sessions. First, we ask a few short questions. This is called a prescreen.

Click Here for Intake

Medication Assistance — FREE

We help you find medicine you can pay for. We send you information for free. Need help to fill out the form? We ask for a donation. We also check if we can help you in one of our programs, and we email you what we find.

Click Here for Intake

Life Insurance Consultation and Quote — FREE

A clear talk about how to protect your family. Your talk and your quote are free. You pay only if you say yes to a policy. Fill out the intake, then book your appointment.

Book an Appointment

Credit Repair — $149.00

We look at your credit and make 1 dispute for you. Price: $149.00 + 3.5% Square fee = $154.22 total. To start, pay, then fill out the intake and upload the items you need.
