Services

Southern Oak Financial & Wellness helps families and children navigate toward a better way of living. We help you protect your family with life insurance, find low-cost medication you can afford, repair your credit for a better living, and file for Social Security (SSA) benefits — for your child, and for your adult child transitioning into adulthood (ages 18–26).

Proudly serving San Antonio, the Rio Grande Valley, and surrounding cities.

A small business owned company with a big heart.

SSA Process of Application

Step-by-step help to apply for SSI, SSDI, or DAC benefits — for a child, or for a young adult who is turning 18.

Life Insurance — Covering Life, In Living and In Loss

Term and IUL coverage with Living Benefits. No fee until you approve your policy.

Credit Repair — Fix Your Credit, Step by Step

We review your credit report, dispute what is wrong, and guide you step by step — for a better living.

Medication Assistance — Prescription Assistance Program

We check if your medication is on our low-cost list. Forms are free if your medication is on the list.

Not sure which one fits your family? Book your free consultation — or send us a referral and we will connect with you.
