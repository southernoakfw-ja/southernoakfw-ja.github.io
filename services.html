Our Services

Help for families in San Antonio, the Rio Grande Valley, and nearby cities. All visits are online.

A small business with a big heart.

What We Do

Social Security Administration Application Guidance — We help you step by step to apply for SSI, SSDI, and DAC. Free. You get 2 free 30-minute sessions. Need more help? It is $10.00 for each 30 minutes more.
Medication Assistance — We help you find medicine you can pay for. Free. We send you information. Need help to fill out the form? We ask for a donation.
Life Insurance Consultation and Quote — A clear talk about how to protect your family. Free. You pay only if you say yes to a policy.
Credit Repair — We look at your credit and make 1 dispute for you. $149.00.

We help 20 people a month with the free services. Book a visit to get on the list. We tell you your number. When it is your turn, we send you an email.

Donations of $10.00 or more are welcome. They help us help more families. Thank you.

For medication help, we also check if we can help you in one of our programs. We send you an email with what we find.

How to Start

Fill out the intake form. Tap the button for the help you need. Then book your visit — or send us your questions. Only credit repair costs money. You pay when you start.

For Social Security Administration help, we ask some questions first. This is called a prescreen. It helps us see if you can apply. See the prescreen questions — one set for a child (17 and under), and one set for 18 and up.

Credit repair rules: Do not pay late. Do not let a store check your credit to buy something. Do not take out new loans. If you do, we must stop your service. The agreement tells you what you need to start.
