Financial

Life Insurance

Protect the people who count on you. Term and IUL plans for parents and children, with Living Benefits — money while you are alive if you get very sick or hurt. We teach you first, in plain words, in English and Spanish.

The 3 Shields: 1) A parent dies — the policy pays the family. 2) A parent gets sick or hurt — Living Benefits replace income and pay for care. 3) The child's future — insurance that funds a special needs trust and keeps SSI and Medicaid safe.

Credit Repair

We check your credit reports with you, find errors, and dispute them for you.

Why your family needs good credit: to rent or buy a home near the care your child needs; lower deposits and lower car and home insurance; many jobs check credit before they hire; to borrow at a fair cost when life happens; less money stress on the whole family.
