Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Southern Oak Financial & Wellness ("Southern Oak," "we," "us") is a small Texas business run by Jessica Alvarez. This page explains, in plain words, what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

What we collect

When you fill out a form on this website, book an appointment, or send your information through a Facebook or Instagram ad form, we may receive:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • Your date of birth and basic details you choose to share for a life insurance quote (such as coverage amount, tobacco use, height and weight, and health notes you provide)
  • The best time to reach you, and any message you write to us

We collect only what we need to answer you and prepare the help you asked for.

How we use it

  • To call or email you back about what you asked for — for example, a free consultation or a life insurance quote
  • To prepare your quote or service with the insurance carriers and partners needed to do the work (life insurance is offered through Premier Financial Alliance / National Life Group)
  • To keep simple records of our work together

What we never do

  • We never sell your information.
  • We never share your information with marketers.
  • We never ask for your Social Security number, bank account, or card numbers through this website, email, or a social media form. Do not send them that way.

Ads and social media

Some people reach us through a Facebook or Instagram ad. When you send your name and phone number through one of those forms, Meta (Facebook) passes your information to us so we can call you back. Meta's own privacy policy also applies to information you share on its platforms.

How we protect it

Your information is kept in the business email and records of Southern Oak and is only seen by Jessica Alvarez. No system is perfect, but we treat your information the way we would want our own treated — carefully and privately.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to update or delete your information, or to stop contacting you. Just email southernoakfw@gmail.com and we will take care of it.
