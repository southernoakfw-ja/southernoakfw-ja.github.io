SSA Child Starter Kit

We are not the Social Security Administration. We are only advocates to help you.

This free kit shows you what to gather. Fill out the short form, and we will email you the next step.

For Texas families only, please.

What to gather before you apply:

  • Your child's birth certificate and Social Security card
  • Doctors, hospitals, and therapists — names, addresses, phone numbers, and dates
  • A list of your child's medicines
  • School records — IEP or 504 plan, and report cards
  • Teachers' names and how your child does in school
  • Medical records you already have
