Watch & Learn

Learn together, as a family — free videos in plain words.

How to watch as a family

1. Watch together

Sit down with your spouse or partner, phones down. Each video is a family talk, not homework.

2. Pause & talk

Every video has family questions under it. Pause, answer out loud together — there are no wrong answers.

3. Take the family step

Each video ends with one small step. Do it together before you close the page.

Start here — Video 1: Life Insurance, in plain words

The full guide: what life insurance is, Term and IUL, and how families pick what fits.

Pause & talk together:

• Who depends on our paycheck?

• If that paycheck stopped for 3 months, what would we pay first?

• Do we want protection for a set number of years, or for our whole lives?

Family step: Write down the names of everyone who depends on your income. That list is who life insurance protects.

Video 2: Living Benefits — life insurance you can use while you’re living

Most families have never heard of this: some policies can pay you while you are still living, if you get very sick.

Pause & talk together:

• If one of us got very sick and could not work, how would the bills get paid?

• Did we know life insurance can pay while you are still living?

Family step: Say your “if I get sick” plan out loud to each other. If you don’t have one yet, that’s okay — that is what Jessica helps families build.

Video 3: Myth — ‘Life insurance is too expensive’

A short cartoon that answers the biggest myth families believe.

True or false? A healthy young parent can often get term life insurance for about the cost of one family pizza night a month.

Tap to check your answer

True for many families. Your real number depends on age and health — and a quote is free, so you never have to guess.

Family step: Get your family’s real number. No guessing, no pressure.

The Family 5-Minute Check

Answer these four together, out loud:

1. If one paycheck stopped tomorrow, how many months could we pay the bills?

2. Do we know what our work coverage really pays — and that it usually ends when the job ends?

3. Do we have a plan if one of us gets very sick and cannot work?

4. Do the adults who love our kids know what our wishes are?

If any answer was “we don’t know” — that’s normal. That’s exactly what a free talk with Jessica is for.
