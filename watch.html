How to Apply for SSA Benefits

Watch how to apply, step by step — one video for a child (17 and under), one for an adult (18 and over). Jessica explains each step herself, in plain words. Recording soon — the steps are below so you can start today.

Video 1 — For a Child (17 and under). A parent or guardian applies for the child. Recording soon.

Video 2 — For an Adult (18 and over). The adult applies, with a helper sitting beside them if they want one. Recording soon.

The steps, in order:

  1. Create your ID.me. Social Security signs you in with a partner account (ID.me or Login.gov). Have ready: your email, your phone, your government photo ID (driver's license, state ID, or passport), your Social Security number, and a phone or computer with a camera for a picture of your ID and a selfie.
  2. Gather your documents and do the free Starter Kit. Birth certificate, Social Security card, doctors and dates seen, medicines, and school papers (for a child) or your jobs for the last 15 years (for an adult). Then fill out the free Starter Kit that fits — Child (17 and under) or Adult (18 plus) — and we email you the next step.
  3. Start the online application. Sign in at ssa.gov and start the application. Answer with what is true today, use real examples of a normal day, and write down any re-entry number the site gives you.
  4. Wait for Social Security to respond. Watch your mail every day. Social Security may call, send forms, or schedule a doctor exam. Answer before every deadline. Waiting is normal — a quiet mailbox does not mean no.
