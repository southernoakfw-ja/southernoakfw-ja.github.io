Credit Repair, Step by Step

What it is:
Step-by-step help to understand your credit reports, spot errors, and dispute what is wrong — so your family can work toward a better living, like qualifying for a home or a car.

How we get started:

We talk first about your goals and whether credit repair is a good fit for you.
You pull all three of your credit reports, free, from AnnualCreditReport.com.
You gather your photo ID, proof of your address, and your three reports.
I review your reports with you and show you what can be disputed and why.
You sign and mail your own dispute letters — you stay in control of your credit.

Please know:
No one can legally remove true, accurate information from a credit report, and no honest service can promise a higher score. What I promise is clear steps, honest review, and help you can follow.
